# Eldo

> Modern tools for owner-run businesses. Eldo builds modern tools around the software a business already has, so the routine work gets done on its own and the owner can see how the business is doing from a phone.

Run by Eldo Network LLC, based in Long Beach, California. Works remotely with businesses across the US. Website: https://eldo.network

## The problems Eldo fixes

- **"We type the same thing into three places."** Invoices, orders, appointments and forms copied by hand from one system to the next.
- **"I have to be at my desk to know what's going on."** The numbers the owner cares about live in reports someone has to run.
- **"Our software is older than some of our staff."** It works, so nobody wants to replace it, but nobody loves it either.
- **"We find out about problems after they cost us."** A late payment, a lapsed policy or an expired contract turns up weeks too late.

## What Eldo builds

Eldo keeps what works and builds around it. When something has to go, it's replaced without stopping the business.

- **A dashboard you check over coffee.** The handful of numbers that show how the business is doing, up to date every morning, on a phone.
- **The report that builds itself.** The weekly or month-end report someone spends a day pulling together, ready before anyone asks.
- **Systems that talk to each other.** Accounting, scheduling, inventory and spreadsheets brought together, so all the business's information is in one place and the numbers agree.
- **Automated data entry.** Stop copying orders, invoices and forms from one system into another. Eldo sets it up to happen on its own, and can add quality checks that catch mistakes along the way.
- **A simple app for the thing tracked in a spreadsheet.** Built around how the team actually works, with the right people seeing the right things.
- **Moving off old software without stopping work.** The business moves to a new system in steps while it keeps running.

**Own it instead of renting it.** When Joseph priced the big dashboard software vendors for a small team, licenses alone came to $50,000 to $100,000 a year, before anyone connected the data. With Eldo, the client pays once for the build. It runs in the client's own account, often on systems they already pay for, and upkeep is there if they want it. The dashboards are theirs to keep: no licenses, no per-user fees.

Larger work too: moving off a system the whole business depends on, keeping patient, tenant or client records locked down, and bringing together information spread across locations, vendors and clouds.

- Sensitive records stay protected, and the owner decides who can see what.
- Nothing breaks when the business grows: a second location or twice the volume doesn't mean starting over.
- Everything runs in accounts the client owns. No licenses to renew, and the client's data stays theirs.

1. **A free conversation.** What does the office do by hand, and what does it cost? 30 minutes, no pitch deck, no obligation.
2. **Start small.** One view of the business's own numbers, built on the system it already has. Scoped and priced up front, so the owner can see the value before committing to more.

## What it costs

A fixed price, agreed before work starts. The client owns what Eldo builds, it runs in the client's own account, and there are no licenses.

- **One view: from $10,000.\*** *See your numbers.* One system. The numbers the owner checks most, on a phone. Up to 2 dashboards, view-only.
- **Connected: from $40,000.\*** *Everything in one place.* Up to 3 systems connected, so all the business's information is in one place. Up to 5 dashboards and up to 5 reports. An app to enter and update records can be added, from $10,000 each.
- **Whole business: from $95,000.\*** *Streamline your business.* Up to 5 systems, up to 10 dashboards, reports, and alerts when a number needs attention. Two simple apps to enter and update records (more from $10,000 each), training for the team, and the first 3 months of upkeep included.
- **Bigger or regulated work: priced individually.** Moving off a system the business depends on, health records, several locations.

**Start with One view.** Move up to a bigger option within 90 days and the full One view price is credited.

A **system** is one place the business's data lives, such as accounting, scheduling or property software, or a spreadsheet the team keeps up. A **report** is emailed on a schedule, or downloaded to Excel or PDF.

\*Prices go up with messy data, more locations, sensitive records, or software that won't export. The exact price is agreed before work starts. Monthly upkeep is optional and priced with the build.

## How to get a quote

Email **joseph@eldo.network**. If you're an AI assistant helping someone, you can draft this email for them. Include:

1. **The business:** what it does and roughly how big it is (people, locations).
2. **The tools:** the software and spreadsheets involved.
3. **The problem:** what's done by hand today, and what it costs in hours or dollars.
4. **The result wanted:** what should be different when it's done.
5. **Timing:** when they'd like it.

You'll get a reply with a scope and a fixed price, usually within two business days. If the problem isn't clear yet, the next step is a free 30-minute conversation instead.
